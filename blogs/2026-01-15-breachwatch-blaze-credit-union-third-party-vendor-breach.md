TL;DR Blaze Credit Union reports a third-party breach tied to Marquis Software Solutions (marketing services). Data including SSNs may have been accessed, impacting ~235,000 members. A multi-month gap between detection and confirmed impact highlights how vendor incidents can expand risk beyond your internal perimeter.
