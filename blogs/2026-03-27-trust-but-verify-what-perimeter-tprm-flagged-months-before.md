We Flagged the Risk.Six Months Before the Stryker Breach. On September 13, 2025, Perimeter’s Monitor module flagged Stryker Corporation as “High Risk” with an F-grade security score of 32%, across 1,905 monitored domains and 5,971 identified risks. Six months later, the breach arrived.
